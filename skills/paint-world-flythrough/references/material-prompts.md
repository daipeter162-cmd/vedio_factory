# 全片材质生成提示词

下列提示词为本项目复现设计，未声称恢复参考作者原文。原画仅作为色板与筆触参考；所有生成素材均通过内置图像生成工具制作。

## 建筑立面

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a flat 2x2 texture atlas for four actual 3D waterfront building facades, NOT a finished scene or concept perspective. Input image is a palette and oil brushwork reference only. Exactly four equally sized square tiles, zero gaps, no borders, no text, no objects outside the tiles. Each tile fills its entire square with one flat FRONT ORTHOGRAPHIC 19th-century French riverside facade: no perspective, no sky, no ground, no roof, no surrounding scenery. Upper left: two-storey muted blue-grey plaster facade with 4 small irregular windows, dark slate shutters, a low ochre door. Upper right: two-storey olive-grey stone facade with two upper windows and an arched ochre door below. Lower left: three-storey grey teal facade, three tiny warm lamp windows and two dark windows, rough doorway at bottom. Lower right: two-storey subdued ochre plaster facade, asymmetric slate green shutters and dark blue doorway. Facades must feel hand painted, tangible layered short oil marks, dark expressive brush outlines around edges, shutters, windows and doors, subtle paint highlights on plaster. Windows mostly dark navy, only a few subdued warm gold lit interiors. Lighting flat and diffuse, no cast shadows or baked photographic lighting. Match reference's muted Prussian blue, grey teal, olive, ochre and restrained pale warm gold. Avoid regular grids, perfect straight lines, photographic brick textures, glossy 3D rendering, modern fixtures, signage. Museum-quality coherent oil illustration. These are rectangular surface maps, not separate cutout houses.

## 屋顶、石岸、地面、树皮

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: seamless 2x2 oil-paint material atlas, full square image. Input painting is style and subdued palette reference only. Exactly 4 equal square panels, no separators, no border, no text, no scenery, no objects. Top left tile: dark blue-grey and desaturated teal roof slate, irregular overlapping short tile shapes outlined by rich dark painterly strokes, subtle olive highlights. Top right: rough stone embankment, navy teal blue-green stones, irregular varied masonry sizes with dark hand painted gaps, no regular brick grid. Bottom left: warm muted olive ochre sandy quay path with short layered flat directional brushstrokes and a few grey-blue paint accents, no cracks or objects. Bottom right: midnight blue and olive green tree bark, thick expressive vertical curving oil strokes, darker crevices, fine ochre blue highlights. ALL are hand-painted oil surfaces with tangible controlled impasto, not photos of real materials, not a digital noisy filter. Match source night palette, no neon or bright yellow. Consistent brush scale and lighting, edge to edge repeatable patterns.

## 船板

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: flat square oil-painted wooden fishing boat surface texture. Reference used solely for subdued night colour palette and brushwork. Entire canvas covered by broad horizontal dark wood plank bands, no perspective, no scenery, no boat silhouette, no borders or text. Hand painted dark Prussian blue-green and deep olive-grey timbers with short warm olive and muted ochre brush highlights, irregular dark horizontal seams, a few subtle small hand painted nail dots, worn painted edges. Warm highlights less than 15% surface. Brushwork tangible, deliberate, small flat oil strokes and ridges follow plank direction; fully painterly and not photo wood grain. About 8 horizontal plank bands across image, varied widths, rough expressive outlines, softened irregular band edges; side edges should tile seamlessly. Lighting flat, no shadows or long gradients. This will wrap a curved actual boat hull and needs rich paint detail without sharp digital noise.

## 透明枝叶

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one isolated hand-painted leafy branch cluster on a genuinely transparent background for layered 3D tree foliage in an oil-paint river night film. Style reference supplied is Van Gogh Starry Night over the Rhone, reference only for muted palette and oil brush handling. Create a roughly rounded irregular spray of dense small olive-blue-green leaves with a few thin dark branches, with tangible thick small oil paint strokes following leaf direction. Rich variations from deep slate teal to desaturated olive to grey sage and occasional restrained ochre edge highlights. Organic fuzzy broken outline, many tiny natural transparent gaps between leaves, no rectangle silhouette. Keep most foliage middle value and readable on a dark blue sky, avoid all-black shadows or neon green. No tree trunk, no roots, no landscape, no sky, no ground, no text, no border. The entire object floats alone on transparent alpha, no cast shadow. Professional painterly branch foliage asset, NOT a low poly 3D render, not photorealistic, not outlined cartoon shapes. Branch cluster fits within square image with at least 5 percent transparent padding on every side.

## 天空材质：assets/painted-sky.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a wide painted night-sky background texture for a true 3D film, not a finished scene. Use the supplied Van Gogh Starry Night over the Rhone ONLY as palette and brushwork reference. Create a completely new wide panoramic sky painting, 3:1 aspect ratio if possible. Entire canvas is sky only, no river, water, boats, buildings, trees, people, border, text, or watermark. Lower quarter: luminous desaturated blue-green and teal twilight night gradient with richly layered small impasto marks. Upper three quarters: deep Prussian blue, cobalt, slate blue and blue green, flowing softly curved and short flat brushstrokes; retain the muted, subtle color harmony of the reference, do not make a bright neon sky. Scatter about 15 distinct small warm pale yellow stars with restrained irregular radial brush halos, larger stars about 3 percent of canvas height, majority much smaller. NO giant spiral swirls, NO moon, NO astrophotography. Individual paint strokes should be fine, tangible oil paint ridges, careful deliberate painterly variation, not noisy digital texture, not polygonal shapes. Spread stars with organic varied spacing across the width, upper sky darker, bottom stays empty and quieter so a 3D city can be layered in front. Professional museum oil-paint surface character suitable for close film viewing. Keep bright yellow area under 3 percent total image.

## 表面笔触：assets/painted-surface.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: seamless square hand painted oil paint surface texture for an animated 3D wooden boat and adapted surface shaders. Use reference solely for muted palette and expressive oil paint handling. Paint a uniform all-over, fine-scale texture in very dark blue-green, Prussian blue, dusty teal, olive-grey and muted ochre grey. Individual clearly visible short flat oil brush marks form loosely horizontal layered rhythms, varied soft bends and occasional diagonal short marks. Dense tactile ridges of paint, delicate grain of canvas barely visible. This is an actual painting of paint marks, not a photograph of wood: NO wood grain, no knots, no plank divisions, no cracks, no objects, no panorama, no scenery, no text, no borders. Avoid streaky noise, digital noise, sharp speckling, long strings, high contrast, neon blue. About 70% of canvas is dark blue-green, 25% muted olive-grey teal, under 5% low-saturation ochre strokes. Palette resembles the dark boat and shore shadows in reference. Fine professional brushwork, consistent density and brightness across all edges, tileable without obvious repeating motifs.
